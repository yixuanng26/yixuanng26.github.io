Observing Real-World IT & Data Operations

Documented infrastructure workflows, security compliance practices, and collaborative agile workspaces observed during our Faculty of Computing industry visit.

1. What I Learned

Physical and cloud security, role-based access control, and clear documentation are inseparable from daily engineering operations.

2. Challenge / Decision

Distilling a full-day tour into concise visual evidence while respecting company confidentiality by focusing strictly on public architectural takeaways.

3. Connection to Course / CLO

Reinforces Ethics, Privacy & Governance in enterprise environments handling sensitive customer transactions.

4. What I Would Improve

Include a comparative diagram contrasting academic dataset workflows with the enterprise CI/CD and staging environments observed.

Explainer Video: End-to-End Data Engineering Lifecycle

Produced an animated/narrated walkthrough illustrating how raw operational events travel through ingestion, storage, transformation, and BI serving.

1. What I Learned

Explaining technical stages visually forced me to clarify the exact boundary between raw data lakes and transformed analytical marts.

2. Challenge / Decision

Keeping the video concise without skipping undercurrents like security and DataOps—solved by using a recurring visual sidebar across scenes.

3. Connection to Course / CLO

Directly demonstrates mastery of the Data Engineering Lifecycle theme and clear technical communication.

4. What I Would Improve

Walk through a concrete e-commerce transaction example from JSON event to SQL aggregate table to ground the theory further.
