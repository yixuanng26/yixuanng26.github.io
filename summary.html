Course Learning Summary

Reflecting on the knowledge gained in SCST1133, how my perspective on data engineering evolved, and where I am heading next.

Before learning SCST1133

Viewing Data as Static Files

Previously, I treated datasets as ready-made CSV files downloaded for standalone Python scripts or SQL exercises—rarely questioning how those tables were captured, governed, or maintained over time.

After learning SCST1133

Systems & Lifecycle Thinking

I now view data engineering as designing trustworthy socio-technical systems: balancing ingestion reliability, schema governance, ethical privacy safeguards, and clear stakeholder serving.

Key Knowledge & Practical Skills
